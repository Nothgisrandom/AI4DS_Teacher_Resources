# Assessment rubric: checking AI statistical claims

Use with the [student activity](../05_Classroom_Activities/Checking_AI_Statistical_Claims_Activity.md). The [teacher lesson](../04_Lesson_Plans/Checking_AI_Statistical_Claims.md) contains the answer guide.

Award 0–3 points per criterion, for a maximum of **12 points**. Allow students to demonstrate understanding in writing, orally, or through an accessible calculation tool.

| Criterion | 3 points | 2 points | 1 point | 0 points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Numerical accuracy | Correct mean and median, with work and units. | Correct results with incomplete work or units. | Shows a relevant method but makes a calculation error. | No relevant calculation. |
| Claim verification | Evaluates all five claims and supports classifications with calculations or clearly identified evidence limits. | Evaluates most claims correctly with some supporting evidence. | Identifies at least one issue but provides little verification. | Accepts the response without checking its claims. |
| Sensitivity and data handling | Correctly recomputes both summaries, explains their differing changes, and states why deletion requires investigation. | Recomputes the summaries and recognizes the large observation’s influence, with a limited discussion of deletion. | Partially analyzes the observation’s influence or recommends deletion without adequate justification. | Does not assess the observation’s effect. |
| Evidence and communication | Gives a clear corrected summary, limits it to the sample, and explains why AI causation is unsupported. | Gives a mostly correct summary and identifies the unsupported causal claim. | Gives a partly corrected summary but retains an unsupported inference or unclear interpretation. | Provides no defensible corrected summary. |

## Feedback prompts

- Which number did you verify independently?
- What evidence supports your interpretation of “typical”?
- What information would help explain the 45-minute attempt?
- What study design or additional data would be needed to investigate whether AI affects completion time?

Treat the point total as a guide for feedback. Adjust grading to your course objectives and communicate any changes before the activity.
